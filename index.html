Common Questions

Before we get started.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Your enquiry can focus on one requirement, such as a website, a training programme, or cloud migration. Related requirements can be discussed as your scope develops.

How are project costs and timelines determined?

Scope, complexity, existing systems, integrations, and delivery requirements affect the estimate. Share your requirements so the proposed scope, timeline, and commercials can be discussed.

Do you provide technical training and eLearning?

Both IT training and eLearning solutions are part of our service offering. Tell us about your audience, learning objectives, preferred format, and technology requirements.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include your business objective, the service you need, any existing systems, and your preferred timeline. Please do not include passwords or sensitive customer information.
